𝐖𝐈𝐓𝐍𝐄𝐒𝐒: Academic excellence took center stage as Holy Cross College of Carigara, Incorporated (HCCCI) held its Recognition and Graduation Day last April 11 and 12.

The ceremony celebrated the achievements of Junior High Completers, Senior High graduates, and College students from first to fourth year. Kent Deloria Reyes, a with high honor graduate and Class Valedictorian from Accountancy, Business and Management (ABM) strand expressed his words of welcome. While, Archie Bald Horca, with high honors and the Class Salutatorian from Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) strand offered the invocation. Ruela Mae Cospada, a Summa Cum Laude graduate in Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (BSBA), delivered her words of gratitude. Meanwhile, Aiyazen Legaspi, a Cum Laude graduate in Bachelor of Physical Education (BPED), pledged allegiance to the alma mater. HCCCI College Dean Sister Maribel Pianco, OSF, in her closing remarks, she emphasized the intrinsic worth of every student, regardless of accolades, stating: “You are always valuable, whether you have medals or none.” The ceremony concluded with the conferral of medals and certificates, formally acknowledging the students' academic diligence and distinction throughout the 2024-2025 academic year. | 𝑳𝒂𝒚𝒐𝒖𝒕 𝒃𝒚 𝑮𝒊𝒓𝒍𝒆𝒆 𝑨𝒏𝒏𝒆 𝑪𝒆𝒓𝒆𝒛𝒐, 𝑺𝑳𝑨 | 𝑪𝒂𝒑𝒕𝒖𝒓𝒆𝒅 𝒃𝒚 𝑫𝒂𝒗𝒆 𝑩𝒂𝒄𝒐𝒏𝒂𝒘𝒂, 𝑹𝑩.

About An Krus

An Krus is the official student publication of Holy Cross College of Carigara Incorporated, established in 1975. We are committed to delivering accurate, timely, and relevant news to the campus community.

Our team of student journalists, photographers, and editors work tirelessly to cover campus events, student life, sports, and important issues affecting our community. We strive to uphold the highest standards of journalism while fostering creativity and critical thinking.
